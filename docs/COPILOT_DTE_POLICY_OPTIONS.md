# Copilot DTE policy — open decision record

**Status:** OPEN; NO DTE POLICY APPROVED  
**Authority effect:** NONE  
**Active production behavior:** unchanged until a separate reviewed deployment

## Settled constraints

- The Phase 0 exact-expiration cap remains 25% of NAV and is enforced per
  calendar expiration date, not per display week.
- Expiration-cycle utilization must enter candidate ranking as well as the
  final admission gate. This design requirement does not choose a tenor.
- Final DTE policy must compare renewal policies over a common horizon with
  common random numbers and a confidence bound on pairwise differences.

## Alternatives still open

| Product | Candidate policy | Status |
|---|---|---|
| CSP | 30–45 DTE | Unapproved candidate |
| Covered call | 7–45 DTE | Unapproved candidate |
| Both | Minimum 14 DTE, maximum 45 DTE | Rejected for now; provisional option only |
| Current CC calculator | 7 / 14 / 21 targets | Unratified legacy behavior, not a mandate |

The proposed 14-DTE floor was not activated because it was offered as an
interim constraint rather than approved as policy. Its rationale remains on
record: 7-DTE renewal economics are currently least reliable, the Phase 0
baseline held 98.49% of observed exposure in one expiration, and isolated
per-cycle normalization does not model state-dependent restarts. The option
may be reconsidered after the renewal comparison; its inclusion here is not a
rejection of 7 DTE on evidence that does not yet exist.

## Cost-model correction preceding tenor research

Execution-cost model `execution-cost-v2` makes two corrections:

1. Covered-call entry fees include both the configured commission and exchange
   fee. The prior expression charged `$0.65` and silently dropped `$0.15` under
   the default configuration.
2. Option entry slippage embedded in `realisticFill` is no longer subtracted a
   second time by `structureCost`. A managed exit still pays its modeled exit
   slippage and fees. Evidence separately records embedded entry, exit, and
   all-in costs.

These changes alter historical CSP scores. Evidence sealed under
`nuvo-model-5.0.0` predates the correction and must not be replayed as though it
were generated by `nuvo-model-5.0.1-execution-cost-v2`. The older evidence is
not rewritten; the model-version boundary explains the expected difference.

absent, 7/14/21 remains the explicit unratified compatibility default so this
precondition change does not silently select a new business.

## Renewal comparison required for a final decision

For tenor `τ` over fixed horizon `H`, compare policies rather than isolated
contracts:

```text
NEV_policy(τ, H)
  = E[sum of cycle rewards]
  − E[sum of execution and state-transition costs]
  − (risk-free rate + hurdle) × committed capital × H
```

Required terminal transitions:

```text
CC expires OTM  -> shares held -> eligible rewrite
CC assigned     -> cash held   -> explicit reacquisition or alternative
CSP expires OTM -> cash held   -> eligible rewrite
CSP assigned    -> shares held -> explicit CC admission or liquidation
```

Cycle count is stochastic. All tenors use the same underlying paths, and the
classifier uses the confidence bound of each pairwise policy difference. Model
output remains provisional until checked against live outcomes.
